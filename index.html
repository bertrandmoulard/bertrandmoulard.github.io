A personal backup tool. It copies Bertrand Moulard's home server configuration to his own Google Drive. It has no other users.

Privacy policy

pi-backup is used only by its owner, Bertrand Moulard. It accesses only the files it creates in his Google Drive (the drive.file scope), stores them encrypted, and shares no data with anyone.
